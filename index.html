Consent matters most for deliverability. Only send to people who opted in — a registration form, a newsletter sign-up, past patients who agreed to updates. Bought or scraped lists are the single biggest reason bulk mail gets flagged, no matter what tooling sends it.

Message

Every send in the queue will automatically get a footer with a real postal/campus address and an unsubscribe link — required by law (CAN-SPAM / NDPR) and expected by spam filters.

Schedule

Sending everyone at once looks like a blast. Splitting into batches — and letting sends land on alternate days rather than all at once — keeps volume looking organic to spam filters.
